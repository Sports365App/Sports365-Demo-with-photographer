FAQ

Questions coaches ask

How does the free trial work?

Every coach and trainer plan comes with 30 days free. No credit card required. Pick 365 Basic, Pro or Elite when you're ready.

What plans are there?

365 Basic starts at $7.99 a month, 365 Pro at $12.99 and 365 Elite at $19.99. Pay every 3 months to save about 15%, or yearly to save about 33%.

What happens if I need more teams or players?

Upgrade anytime and keep all your teams, rosters and schedules. Programs with more than 10 teams can join the 365 Organization waitlist.

I'm a photographer. How does 365 Media work?

Pick 365 Media Basic, Pro or Elite and pass a background check. Coaches and parents can then book you, and on Pro and Elite your galleries go straight to the team's families. Sports365 takes no commission on your bookings.

Do parents and athletes have to pay?

No. Parent and individual athlete accounts are always free.

Can I sync with my Google Calendar?

Yes. Connect Google Calendar from your Sports365 calendar to keep everything in one place.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no contracts. You can cancel your plan at any time.
